Motors

After enabling, lift the robot upright: it starts balancing within 5° of the target and stops by itself past 45°. STOP turns the motors off, so the robot falls.

Drive

Hold a button or WASD / arrow keys. Driving stops 1 s after the page stops sending.

Tuning

Target angle (°) Kp Ki Kd
Target angle: the pitch it balances at, to make up for weight that isn't centered over the axle (+ leans forward, ±10° max). Gains act on radians: Kp = 6 gives 10% duty at about 1° of error. All saved on the server.
